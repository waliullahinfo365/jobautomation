# CV tailoring, cover letters, and ATS scoring

This is the intended product flow in the app.

## CV tailoring

CV tailoring is live:

1. Go to **Quick Review** → open any job card → tap **Tailor CV for this job**.
2. AI rewrites your headline and summary to match that specific job plus ATS keywords (without inventing facts).
3. Download a **styled PDF** (four templates: with or without photo).

## Cover letter PDF

In the **same modal** → **Cover Letter** tab → download a properly formatted PDF (**Modern** or **Classic** style).

## ATS scoring

1. Go to **Documents** → **CV Library**.
2. Tap **ATS Score** on any CV you have uploaded **with extractable or pasted text**.
3. AI scores your CV across several categories and surfaces what to improve.

## Getting started

1. Upload your **general (master) CV** once under **Documents** → workspace library. PDF and Word are read automatically; you only need to paste text if the file has no extractable text (e.g. some scanned PDFs).
2. Open any job in **Quick Review** and choose **Tailor CV for this job**. Extra instructions in the modal are **optional** — leave them blank for default tailoring to that role.

Your career facts do not change; the product only reframes wording and emphasis per job and ATS keywords.
